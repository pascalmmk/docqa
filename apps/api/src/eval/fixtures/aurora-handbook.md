# Aurora Robotics — Employee Handbook (excerpt)

## Time off

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly. Unused vacation rolls over up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year. Vacation requests longer than two consecutive weeks require sign-off from a department head.

Sick leave is unlimited but requires a doctor's note after the fifth consecutive day. Public holidays follow the calendar of the employee's contracting office, either Berlin or Lisbon.

## Parental leave

Aurora provides 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for all new parents, regardless of gender, usable within the first 12 months after birth or adoption. A phased return option allows working 60% hours at full pay for the first four weeks back.

## Compensation and benefits

Salaries are benchmarked yearly against the 65th percentile of the Radford index for the EU robotics sector. The company pension plan matches employee contributions up to 4% of gross salary. Every employee receives a hardware budget of €1,800 renewed every 24 months.

The on-call stipend is €450 per week carried, paid in addition to base salary. On-call rotations may not exceed one week in four.

## Expenses

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to €75 per person per day, receipts required. Flights under six hours must be booked in economy class. Taxi rides are reimbursable only when public transport would exceed 60 minutes door-to-door.

## Performance and promotion

Promotion cycles run twice a year, with packets due in March and September. Each packet needs a self-review, a manager review, and two peer reviews. New hires have a probation period of 90 days, with a mid-point check-in at day 45.

## Security

All employees must use a hardware security key (YubiKey 5C) as a second factor for company systems. Laptops must run full-disk encryption and auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Sharing production credentials in chat tools is a fireable offense.
